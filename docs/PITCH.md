# Pitch (5 minutes)

**Hook (30 s).** Squirrels forget most of the acorns they bury, and the ones they forget grow into oaks. Forests are planted by forgetting. In *Forgotten Acorns* you are that squirrel for one year.

**Live play (2 min 30 s).** One person plays; another narrates over it. With the season length at 1:00, a whole year fits in four minutes.

1. **Spring:** munch the flowers next to your trees before the bees settle in and build a hive. A hive tree can't be climbed.
2. **Summer:** the dog bursts through the gate, so climb a tree. The hawk is just a screech and a blur, so get under the leaves.
3. **Fall:** acorns drop. Watch the squirrel fluff up as it over-eats. A rival peeks out of a tree, so chase it off before burying.
4. **Winter:** dark and snowing. Follow the sparkles to your stashes, read the fox's prints, and hide in a burrow to get warm.
5. **Spring again:** your forgotten stashes pop up as sprouts. That's the score.

**The theme is the rules (1 min).** Every season changes what you can do (bury or not, climb or not, what eats you, how far you see), and every season's choices carry into the next:

- Flowers you didn't eat become hives.
- Acorns you left on the ground are lost to the snow.
- Fat from fall keeps you alive in winter.
- What you forget becomes next year's trees.

**Accessibility and craft (30 s).** Flip on high contrast (threats get shape-coded markers) and show the remap screen. All the art, sound and music were generated in code during the jam.

**Close (30 s).** Show the end screen: "The stashes you forgot are growing." Keep going and the park becomes a forest.

## Likely judge questions

- *Why does the hawk never appear?* It's a predator you never see coming. You learn to read the screech and the shadow, and you learn where the safe crowns are.
- *What stops hoarding?* Your cheeks hold 4 acorns. Ground acorns vanish when the snow comes. Rivals take whole stashes. The ground freezes in winter, and fall has fewer acorns than you'd like.
- *Why is a big stash risky?* It's easy to find again, but a rival takes all of it at once, and if you forget it, it's still only one tree.
- *How do you know it's balanced?* A bot (`-- --bot`) plays whole years at 4× speed and reports how close it came to starving or freezing. We tuned against it, and it found real bugs.
