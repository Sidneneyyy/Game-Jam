Forgotten Acorns

You are a red squirrel. Bury acorns to survive the winter. Every cache you never dig up becomes an oak, and oaks feed you next year.

SpringRain. Leftover caches sprout when it ends.
SummerHeat and a hawk. Hide in oak shade.
FallAcorns drop. Bury them fast.
WinterFrozen ground. Find caches by scent.

Survive 3 years to see your grove. Best grove: 0 oaks.
