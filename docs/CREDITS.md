# Credits

## Made during the jam by the team

- Game design, code, and every sprite, tile, icon and animation (generated by `tools/draw_*.py`)
- The 5×7 pixel font (`tools/draw_ui.py`)
- All sound effects and the five music loops, synthesised from scratch (`tools/make_sounds.py`)

No third-party art, sound, music or fonts are used.

## Third-party tools and libraries

| Tool | Used for | License |
|---|---|---|
| [Godot Engine 4.7.2](https://godotengine.org) | Game engine, Windows and web export templates | MIT |
| [Python 3](https://www.python.org) | Asset generation scripts | PSF License |
| [Pillow](https://python-pillow.org) | Writing PNG sprites and sheets | MIT-CMU (HPND) |
